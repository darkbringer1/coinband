Walk-forward backtest of Timestral forecasts on Binance pairs
Unofficial. Not affiliated with Mistral AI or Binance. Not financial advice.

Each origin forecasts horizon bars from the context bars before it. A new forecast is made every stride bars (1 = every bar, so the cone moves with the price). The last one starts at the last closed candle: a live forecast. Max origins caps how many bars are replayed. Trend window > 0 adds the recent drift to every forecast; the plain forecast is scored next to it.
